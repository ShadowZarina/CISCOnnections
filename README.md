# CISCOnnections
Inspired by Obsidian's connection webs, CISCOnnections provides an effective way to connect thoughts, bringing together names, categories, and notes through relationships in a 3D graph.
<br><br>
Access the website live through [CISCOnnections](link)!

## Features
CISCOnnections is the perfect tool for visual and writing-based planners who love basking in the wonders of space! Create galaxies for each of your notebooks, add categories through planets of various colors and shapes, forge stars into existence by listing down names, add your own notes and descriptions as surrounding moons and comets, and connect them all to form your very own constellations!
